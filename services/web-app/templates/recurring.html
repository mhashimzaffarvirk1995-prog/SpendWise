{{ icon('info',16) }} How schedules work

Due entries are added every minute while SpendWise is running. Missed dates catch up after restart, up to 100 entries per cycle. Dates use UTC.

Monthly schedules keep the original day, using the last day for shorter months. Resuming includes missed dates. Deleting a schedule keeps existing transactions.
